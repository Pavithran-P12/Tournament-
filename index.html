    How the day runs

    Teams play in groups, then the knockouts:

    • Beginner Men's: 4 groups of 6, everyone plays everyone (5 matches each). The top two of each group play the quarter-finals.
    • Beginner Women's: 1 group of 6, everyone plays everyone (5 matches each). The top two play the final.
    • Intermediate Men's: 4 groups of 5, everyone plays everyone (4 matches each). The top two of each group play the quarter-finals.
    • Intermediate Mixed: 4 groups of 4, everyone plays everyone. The top two of each group play the quarter-finals.

      Scoring

      Rally scoring with a golden point: there is no deuce, so at one point from the target, the next rally wins the game.

      • League: one game to 15. At 14–14 the next rally wins.
      • Quarter-finals: one game to 21. At 20–20 the next rally wins.
      • Semi-finals and finals: best of 3 games to 21. At 20–20 the next rally wins the game.
      • Group ranking: matches won, then head-to-head, then point difference, then points scored.

      What's included

      Every team gets these on the day. Fixtures are shared with all teams 24 hours before play.
