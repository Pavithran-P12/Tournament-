    How the day runs

    Teams are drawn into groups of four, with a few groups of three. Everyone plays everyone in their group, then the four group winners in each category play the semi-finals.

      Scoring

      Rally scoring, one game per match.

      • League: first to 15. At 14–14 a team must lead by 2, up to a maximum of 21.
      • Semi-finals and finals: first to 21. At 20–20 a team must lead by 2, up to a maximum of 30.
      • Group ranking: matches won, then head-to-head, then point difference, then points scored.
