Privacy

Short version: player names are shown only to run the tournament. We do not collect contact details, there are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking, just an anonymous visit count.

Last updated 24 October 2026

Player names

Team names and pairings come from the organiser's entry list. They appear on the schedule, group tables, match pages and result images for one purpose: running and following this tournament.

Names are not linked to phone numbers, email addresses, ages, photos or any other details. The site does not hold those details at all.

Scores and results

Scores are entered courtside by the organiser's scorers and shown live to everyone. They are kept in our live scores database (Supabase) as the match code, the points, the court and the time of the last update. No names or personal details are stored there; team names come from the public draw on this site.

No accounts, cookies or tracking

There is nothing to sign up for. The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, ads or tracking pixels, only an anonymous visit count.

A few settings stay in your own browser only, never sent anywhere: scores a scorer has entered that have not been sent yet, and, on a scorer's tab only, the unlocked passkey until the tab is closed or locked, and a note that you already sent feedback. The visit counter's random ID is the only thing sent (see below).

Visit counter

The footer shows how many visits the site has had. Each browser gets a random ID that stays on that device, and it is counted at most once a day. Only that random ID, the date and the page path are stored, in our database at Supabase. No name, IP address or other personal detail is kept.

Feedback form

The feedback form asks no name, email or phone number. Only your answers and the page you sent it from are emailed to the organisers, through the form service Web3Forms. Please don't type personal details in the comment box.

Result images

Share images are drawn on your own phone. Nothing is uploaded unless you choose to share or save the image yourself.

Services the site loads

Like any website, a few providers see basic technical data such as your IP address when the page loads: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts and Google Maps (fonts and the venue map). We don't receive or keep that data.

Want your name changed or removed?

Message the organisers on Instagram at @thirdspace_by_losh and it will be corrected or taken off the site.
